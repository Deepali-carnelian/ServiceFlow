# Customer Persona — Small Service Business Operator

## Business context

The discovery call describes a small commercial refrigeration repair business with an owner/operator, part-time bookkeeping support and four field technicians. Roughly 15–20 new requests arrive each week plus repeat work.

## Current workflow

Requests arrive through office calls forwarded to a mobile phone, website-form emails, text messages, repeat customers/referrals and handwritten notes. The primary problem is not request volume; it is **lost follow-up context** across scattered channels.

## Core requirement

The operator needs one place to answer two questions:

1. What needs attention today?
2. Where does every active job stand?

## Scope decision

The initial product should not become a full field-service management suite. Route optimization, invoicing, accounting, inventory, detailed service milestones and broad dispatch are later possibilities only if future discovery creates a reason to build them.

Multi-channel capture and calling are included only because they directly reduce lead leakage and keep the same job record current.
